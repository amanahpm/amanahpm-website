About Amanah Property Management

With over thirty-eight years of experience in Metro Detroit real estate, our job is to make property straightforward for everyone involved — whether you are looking for your next home, or you own a building and would rather not spend your evenings on it.

We work across Dearborn, Detroit, Downriver and western Wayne County, and we are in the same neighborhoods as the properties we manage.

What we manage

We offer a deep wealth of experience in managing a wide range of property types — including single-family homes, multifamily units, and commercial spaces — each requiring tailored strategies to maximize value and efficiency. Our team is adept at handling the unique demands of each property class, ensuring optimal occupancy, compliance, and long-term asset performance.

Who we work with

We take pride in servicing a diverse group of investors, from first-time landlords to large-scale portfolio holders. By understanding and adapting to each investor’s goals and risk tolerance, we are able to deliver personalized service, transparent communication, and data-driven results that build lasting trust and success.

For residents

A range of homes, apartments and commercial units across the area, kept in a condition we would be willing to live in ourselves. Apply online, pay rent online, and submit a maintenance request with photos that goes to a crew rather than a voicemail box.

For owners

Marketing and showings, tenant screening against one written standard, rent collection with late fees actually enforced, turnovers and repairs by our own crew, city registration and inspections kept current, and monthly statements you can read without calling to ask what a line means.
